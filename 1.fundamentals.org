* ($\S1.1$) A Simple 1-D Boundary-Value Problem
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear appear appear ...)
- A boundary-value problem consists of a (linear only, for our purposes and scope) partial differential equation, subject to a dual set of initial and final boundary conditions. The solution of the dependent variable at the final state is sought.
- \[ u_{,xx} + \mathscr{f} = 0\]
  (comma stands for differentiating)
- \[ \mathscr{f}:[0,1] \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \]
  is a load function mapping in the domain interval.
- The main constituents to be derived in finite element method are
  + The /variational/ or /weak/ form of the problem, and
  + introduction of the approximate solution space
- What is the physical relevance of this problem? (longitudinal motion in elastic rod, displacement of a taught string, etc.)
- What are /open/ and /closed/ *domains*, $\Omega$, $\bar\Omega$, and *boundaries*, $\partial\Omega^-$, $\partial\Omega^+$ in one-d?
** ($\S1.2$) Strong, or Classical Form
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear appear appear ...)
- The strong form of the boundary-value problem, $(S)$, is stated as follows:
- Given $\mathscr{f}:\bar\Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ and constants $\mathscr{g}$ and $\mathscr{h}$, find $u: \bar\Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, such that
  \begin{align}
  u_{,xx} + \mathscr{f} &= 0 \hspace{1em} \textrm{on}\,\Omega \nonumber\\
  u(1) &= \mathscr{g} \hspace{1em} \textrm{on}\,\partial\Omega^- \nonumber\\
  -u_{,x}(0) &= \mathscr{h} \hspace{1em} \textrm{on}\,\partial\Omega^+ \nonumber
  \end{align}
