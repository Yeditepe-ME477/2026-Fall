Goal

  • The undergraduate level course serves as an introduction to the theory of finite elements by providing coverage of topics from the linear theory.
  • The graduate level course will focus on the mathematical derivation of the finite element method.

Contents

  • Basic concepts of displacement-based finite element method, generalization of element coordinates, implementation in computer programs, formulation and calculation of isoparametric and structural elements are covered as well as numerical integrations. The solution of equilibrium equations in stationary mechanical systems as well as modal response of structures are considered.

CLOs

  1. Gain aptitude for reading and writing mathematical expressions in tensor indicial notation.
  2. Relate and transform between the variational and differential forms of a boundary-value problem.
  3. Identify and use appropriate finite elements in modeling and discretizing boundary-value problems.
  4. Translate the concepts of meshing, boundary conditions and loading, solution algorithms, and post-processing into software input.
  5. Prepare input files to solve for the modal response of structures.
  6. Process the software results from data files to create physically meaningful graphs, charts and tables.
  7. Verify and validate numerical solutions to engineering problems.
