* Part 1: Create a Toy Heat Conduction Problem
- Take a wall of thickness, $w$, subjected to a temperature gradient, due to differing temperatures on its left and right edges, $\mathscr{g}_l$ and $\mathscr{g}_r$, respectively.
- Consider modeling part of the cross-section of the wall by modeling it as a rectangular domain, of size $w \times w$.
- There will be "no heat flux" conditions assigned to its upper and lower edges.
- Assume the wall is made of a particular isotropic material with a given conductivity, $\kappa$.
- We would like to use a finite element model to solve for the "steady-state" temperature distribution.
- Split the domain by a series of longitudinal elements spanning from bottom to the top, say two elements to begin with, and refine systematically to obtain better estimates of the solution (temperature distribution).
