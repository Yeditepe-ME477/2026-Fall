*** Brief Problem Description
A two-dimensional square domain is used to model the cross-section of a solid three-dimensional cylindrical object. The left and bottom edges of the object are being subjected to prescribed temperatures, $\mathscr{g}_l$ and $\mathscr{g}_b$, while the right edge is insulated (no heat flux). Finally the top edge is bounded by a fluid (water) at a uniform temperature, $u_\infty$, which can be modeled using a natural convection condition.
